Looking to update your Capacitor apps quickly and securely without app store delays? Over-the-Air (OTA) updates allow you to push changes directly to users, saving time and bandwidth. Here’s what you need to know:

Key Benefits of OTA Updates:

  • Fast Deployment: Skip app store reviews and deliver updates in seconds.
  • Targeted Updates: Update only modified parts of the app.
  • Rollback Capability: Revert to a previous version if needed.
  • Success Rates: 95% of users receive updates within 24 hours, with an 82% global success rate.

Top Plugins for OTA Updates:

  1. Capgo: Fast updates (5MB in 114ms), end-to-end encryption, real-time analytics, and flexible pricing (starting at $12/month).
  2. Appflow: Enterprise-grade features at $6,000/year but shutting down in 2026.
  3. Microsoft CodePush: Discontinued in 2024.
