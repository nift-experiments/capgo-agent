				302 container How do App Store credentials compare for native builds?
					303 text How do App Store credentials compare for native builds?
				304 text Appflow stores your Apple ID and app-specific password to upload binaries. Capgo uses App Store Connect API keys (.p8): scoped, revocable, and independent of personal Apple ID password changes. For cloud builds, credentials are only used in ephemeral environments — not parked on a vendor server indefinitely.
				305 container Why is Capawesome Ionic's migration partner and not Capgo?
					306 text Why is Capawesome Ionic's migration partner and not Capgo?
				307 text In September 2026 Ionic named Capawesome its preferred migration partner for Appflow and its enterprise plugins. Capgo has never been an Ionic partner. For years we built open alternatives to Ionic's paid products and fixed gaps in the Capacitor ecosystem ourselves, often going further than Ionic's own team, which never made us a fit for their program. Capawesome has always worked the way Ionic prefers. Partner status is a business relationship, not a technical ranking: Capgo and Capawesome have both moved Appflow customers, so compare the products on their merits.
