Launch checklist

How to publish your app

Follow these steps to submit your app. Prepare credentials early to avoid delays during review.

Apple App Store

  1. Create an Apple Developer account and enroll your team.
  2. Prepare certificates, profiles, and app identifiers.
  3. Build and upload your binary with Xcode or CI.
  4. Complete App Store Connect metadata and screenshots.
  5. Submit for review and respond to any feedback.

Google Play

  1. Register a Google Play developer account.
  2. Create your app listing and content ratings.
  3. Upload an AAB build and configure release tracks.
  4. Complete policy declarations and data safety forms.
  5. Publish and monitor initial rollout.

FAQ

Common questions about publishing costs

Still unsure? We can help you plan your release timeline.

How much does it cost to publish an app on the App Store? +

Apple requires an annual developer membership to publish on the App Store. Additional costs depend on your stack, backend, and launch needs.

Do I need to pay Google Play every year? +

No. Google Play charges a one-time developer registration fee for most accounts.

What commission do Apple and Google charge? +

Both stores take a revenue share on paid apps and in-app purchases. Reduced rates may apply to qualifying developers and subscriptions.

Can I update my app without a full store review? +

Store updates go through review, but web-layer changes can be delivered faster with tools like Capgo after your app is approved.
