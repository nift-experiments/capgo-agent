Honest take

Where Capawesome Cloud is the better choice

No tool fits every team. These are the cases where we would point you to the other option.

Lowest entry price

Their Live Updates plans start at $9/month for 1,000 monthly active users, below the Capgo entry plan. [1]

Live updates from a git ref

Point their CLI at a branch or tag and the Cloud Runner clones, builds, and ships the live update. With Capgo you build in your own CI and upload the bundle. [3]

More store destinations

Their store publishing covers TestFlight, Google Play, Huawei AppGallery, and Firebase App Distribution. [2]

Migration path

Thinking of switching?

If you're on Capawesome and considering Capgo, migration is straightforward. The Capacitor update plugin architecture is similar.

  1. Swap the plugin package

    Uninstall @capawesome/capacitor-live-update, install @capgo/capacitor-updater, then run npx cap sync.

  2. Update config (minor differences)

    Set CapacitorUpdater options in capacitor.config.ts and call notifyAppReady() on launch. Channels live in the Capgo dashboard or API.

  3. Update your upload process

    Replace the Capawesome deploy step in CI with npx @capgo/cli@latest bundle upload.

Why is Capawesome Ionic's migration partner and not Capgo?
In September 2026 Ionic named Capawesome its preferred migration partner for Appflow and its enterprise plugins. Capgo has never been an Ionic partner. For years we built open alternatives to Ionic's paid products and fixed gaps in the Capacitor ecosystem ourselves, often going further than Ionic's own team, which never made us a fit for their program. Capawesome has always worked the way Ionic prefers. Partner status is a business relationship, not a technical ranking: Capgo and Capawesome have both moved Appflow customers, so compare the products on their merits.
