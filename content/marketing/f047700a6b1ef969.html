Capawesome Cloud — what it is

Capawesome ships popular Capacitor plugins and a cloud platform for live updates and builds. Many teams evaluate it next to Capgo.

What Capawesome offers

Capawesome Cloud combines plugin maintenance with hosted live updates and CI-style workflows tied to their ecosystem.

How Capgo differs

Capgo is independent, fully open source on the updater, and keeps your CI. Read the detailed comparison — we do not invent pricing or uptime claims beyond that page.
