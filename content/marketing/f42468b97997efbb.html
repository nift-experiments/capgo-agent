Honest take

Where Ionic Appflow is the better choice

No tool fits every team. These are the cases where we would point you to the other option.

Your app ends before 2028

Ionic keeps Appflow running for existing customers until December 31, 2027. If the app will be retired or rebuilt before then, staying may cost less effort than a migration. [1]

You build non-Capacitor apps

Appflow also builds React Native and native iOS and Android apps. Capgo Builder targets Capacitor apps, so those projects need another build service. [2]

You want one hosted CI/CD dashboard

Appflow bundles CI/CD automations in the same dashboard. Capgo does not replace your pipeline: you trigger Capgo from GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins. [2]

Migration path

How hard is migration?

The official guide covers the plugin swap, configuration, and CI changes. Ionic enterprise plugins have direct Capgo replacements listed below.

  1. Install the Capgo updater

    Uninstall @capacitor/live-updates and install @capgo/capacitor-updater.

  2. Replace the sync code

    Call notifyAppReady() instead of the Appflow sync, download, and reload code.

  3. Upload from your CI

    Upload bundles with npx @capgo/cli@latest bundle upload instead of the Appflow CLI.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to change my CI/CD setup?
Nope. Whatever you're using now (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, etc.) keeps working. Just change the upload step to use our CLI instead of Appflow's.
What about native builds?
Yes. Capgo Builder builds and signs iOS and Android binaries in the cloud, typically in 2–3 minutes. Trigger it from the CLI or from your existing CI; Capgo does not replace your CI/CD pipeline.
Will this save money?
Appflow Live Updates started at $499/month and is no longer sold to new customers. Capgo starts at $12/month billed yearly ($14 billed monthly), with a 14-day free trial and no credit card required.
How's the reliability?
Capgo delivers 1B+ updates per month to 90M+ devices. Live status and incident history are public at status.capgo.app.
How do App Store credentials compare for native builds?
Appflow stores your Apple ID and app-specific password to upload binaries. Capgo uses App Store Connect API keys (.p8): scoped, revocable, and independent of personal Apple ID password changes. For cloud builds, credentials are only used in ephemeral environments — not parked on a vendor server indefinitely.
Why is Capawesome Ionic's migration partner and not Capgo?
In September 2026 Ionic named Capawesome its preferred migration partner for Appflow and its enterprise plugins. Capgo has never been an Ionic partner. For years we built open alternatives to Ionic's paid products and fixed gaps in the Capacitor ecosystem ourselves, often going further than Ionic's own team, which never made us a fit for their program. Capawesome has always worked the way Ionic prefers. Partner status is a business relationship, not a technical ranking: Capgo and Capawesome have both moved Appflow customers, so compare the products on their merits.
